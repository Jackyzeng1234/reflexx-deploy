Reaction time, explained

What is a reaction time test?

A reaction time test measures how fast you respond to a visual cue — usually a screen changing colour. It's the standard way to benchmark the speed of your eyes → brain → hand pipeline. On ReflexX the simple test records the milliseconds between the screen turning green and your click, then averages five rounds into one score.
